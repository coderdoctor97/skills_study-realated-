Write a short, friendly sales email to a prospect whose goal is to get them to book a demo call.

1. Draft the email body.
   - Keep it short and friendly.
   - Mention the product name, Trackio.
   - State that the free trial lasts 14 days.
   - Reference that our previous clients saved roughly 20% of their time.
   - End by asking whether they are open to a quick call next week.
2. Write a subject line for the email.

Constraints:
- Do not sound pushy: avoid words such as "act now".
